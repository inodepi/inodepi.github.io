Use cases

After-hours intake

Answer the calls that arrive after your team closes, capture the reason for the call, and book the next open slot.

Lunch and overflow

Pick up the calls a busy team cannot reach, so a ringing phone never becomes a lost customer.

New-customer screening

Collect contact details, the service needed and the reason for the call before a human touches the schedule.

Cancellation recovery

Offer freed slots to callers instead of letting them fall into voicemail.

What it does

  • Answers missed and after-hours calls, 24/7
  • Captures caller intake as structured data (not a transcript to re-read)
  • Conflict-checks Google Calendar and books a real slot
  • Alerts your team with a short summary
  • Logs every call to Google Sheets
  • Configurable services, hours, intake fields and escalation

How it's built

One shared voice layer on Retell, three interchangeable engines. The same receptionist is implemented on Make, n8n and Zapier so a business can keep the stack it already pays for instead of adopting a new one.

The n8n branch runs end to end on synthetic call payloads — real Google Calendar booking, Sheets logging and Gmail alerts. The phone call itself is simulated; live telephony is a documented Retell setup away. Make and Zapier remain simulated.

Compliance

A HIPAA-compliant build is available for practices that will handle real patient information: scoped architecture, BAAs, access controls, audit logging, a PHI-safe infrastructure and vendor review before go-live. Compliance is established per deployment, not claimed by the public demo.
