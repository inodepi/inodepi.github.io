Visit analytics

This site uses Microsoft Clarity to understand which pages people read and where they stop. It records anonymous page views, clicks, scroll depth and rough device/location information. It does not know your name, does not build an advertising profile, and is not connected to your identity. The data is only used to decide what to write or fix next.

What you send me

When you email me or use the Cal.com booking page, you choose what to share — usually a name, a number and what you want automated. I use it only to reply and, if we work together, to do the work. It is never sold, shared with advertisers, or added to a marketing list you didn't ask for.

Email

If you subscribe to post notifications, every email includes a one-click unsubscribe. Unsubscribing adds your address to a suppression list so it is not contacted again. There is no drip sequence and no re-subscribing you.

Tools this site depends on

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site and logs standard server requests, including IP addresses, under GitHub's own privacy policy.
  • Microsoft Clarity provides the visit analytics described above.
  • Cal.com handles booking and has its own privacy policy for the details you enter there.

Removing your data

Ask and it's gone. Email kristiandyanbusiness@gmail.com with "delete my data" and I'll confirm what was removed within a few days. That includes emails, chat history from any work, and analytics cannot be tied back to you individually, so there is nothing personal to delete there.

What this site doesn't do

  • No advertising pixels, retargeting or data brokers.
  • No selling or renting of anything you share.
  • No newsletters you didn't sign up for.
