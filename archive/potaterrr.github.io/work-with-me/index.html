# Before you book

I don't know which tool I need — is that okay?

Yes. Bring the process, not the stack. I'll pick between Make, n8n, Python and the tools you already pay for, and explain why that choice is the cheapest one that works.

Do I need to be technical?

No. You describe the manual work; I handle the build, the documentation and the handover. One revision round is included in every scope.

Who owns the automation and my data?

You do. Everything is built in your accounts and stays exportable. Credentials live in your vault, not mine, and the handover includes setup notes.

How is pricing decided?

There are no prices on the site on purpose. The audit call finds the real scope, then you get one fixed quote — so you pay for the work that removes the loop, not a package guess.

What about clinics, healthcare and sensitive data?

A HIPAA-compliant build is available: human checkpoints, an audit trail, and data handled inside your own accounts. Say so on the call and the scope accounts for it.
